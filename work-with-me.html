Web development and consulting

I take on a limited number of independent web projects.

I help organizations build and maintain websites, plan redesigns, and figure out what their website needs to do for the business. Depending on the project, I can handle the development or help you plan the work and choose the right team.

What I do

Web development

I build custom WordPress sites and websites using HTML and CSS, with an emphasis on accessibility and code that’s straightforward to maintain. My work has ranged from standalone product sites to interactive maps.

Not every project needs a custom build. If a simpler platform fits your needs, I’ll recommend it.

Redesign and migration planning

I help organizations work through the decisions that come before a redesign: what to keep, what to change, which platform to use, and what a development partner needs to deliver.

At Green America, I led planning for a large-scale Drupal-to-WordPress redesign, including the RFP, vendor evaluation, and technical mapping. The work involved more than 15 stakeholders and an external development team.

If you’re moving away from Drupal, replacing a legacy CMS, or figuring out whether you’ve outgrown your current platform, I can help you define the project and evaluate your options.

Digital strategy

Before you spend money on a website, I help you get clear about what it needs to accomplish—whether that’s generating leads, attracting members, increasing registrations, or bringing in sales and donations.

From there, we can work out what to build first, what budget makes sense, and how to measure the results. You can bring me in for that planning work even if someone else will build the site.

Who I work best with

I work best with small businesses, associations, arts and education organizations, and nonprofits that have small teams and need their websites to do useful work.

I’m particularly useful when you need someone who can help set priorities and also work directly in the code. If you’re comparing agencies or proposals and aren’t sure what you need, I can help you sort that out before you commit to a build.

Other projects

Last Supper Sculpture — Built a Squarespace website for an art project. It was the right tool for the project; a custom build wasn’t necessary.
Retreat leader website — Provide ongoing maintenance and updates for a personal practice website.

How it works

Send me an email with a little about your organization, what you need help with, and any timing or budget you already have in mind. You don’t need a finished brief—just a place to start.

I’ll get back to you within two business days, and we’ll figure out whether I’m a good fit. No intake form or required sales call.
